**1\. Investment**

The investor shall invest the amount stated above in the clothing importation and wholesale business operated by \[**the owner\]** under the name and style of **\[\[name of business\]\]**, payable in full on or before the Effective Date. \[**the owner\]** acknowledges receipt of the Investment upon such payment.

**2\. Term and Lock-In**

The Investment is locked in for thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date. The Investor may not withdraw any part of the Investment before the Term ends, except as provided in Sections 9 and 10\.

**3\. Monthly Return**

The Business shall pay the Investor a fixed Monthly Return equivalent to five percent (5%) of the original Investment amount for each applicable month. The Monthly Return shall be calculated based on the original Investment amount only and shall not be compounded. Monthly Returns shall be payable from the third (3rd) month following the Effective Date until the end of the Term, subject to Sections 4 and 5\.

**4\. Deferred Start of Returns**

**\[\[name of business\]\]** imports merchandise from China, and shipments generally take approximately forty-five (45) to sixty (60) days to arrive in the Philippines, depending on shipping schedules, customs processing, and other factors affecting transportation and importation. Upon arrival of the merchandise, \[\[name of business\]\] generally requires an additional one (1) to two (2) weeks, depending on the volume of the items received, to sort, organize, and prepare the merchandise. Once the merchandise has been sorted and prepared, customers are generally given seven (7) days to complete payment for their purchases.

Accordingly, the Investor acknowledges that the Investment is subject to the normal importation, inventory preparation, sales, and customer payment cycle of business. No Monthly Return shall be payable for the first two (2) months following the Effective Date, and the first Monthly Return shall be due for the third (3rd) month following the Effective Date. The Investor acknowledges and accepts this initial deferral as part of the normal business cycle of the clothing wholesale business.

**5\. Annual Chinese New Year (Spring Festival) Period**

Each year, the Chinese New Year (Spring Festival) holiday in China, which usually falls between mid-January to mid-February, results in temporary closures of many suppliers and markets and may disrupt normal shipping schedules and business operations. The effects of this closure continue into the following weeks and months, and the business's import and inventory cycle is affected through the month of March. Accordingly, no Monthly Return shall be payable for the month of March of each year during the Term. The March Monthly Return shall not accrue or be carried over to the following month, and regular Monthly Returns shall resume in April.

**6\. Shipping and Importation Delays**

The Investor understands that shipments from China may occasionally be delayed due to circumstances beyond the reasonable control of **the business**, including random customs inspections, shipping delays, vessel schedules, and port congestion during peak seasons, particularly during November and December. Customs inspections or related importation procedures may, in some cases, take several weeks to month(s) to complete.

Such delays may affect the timing of inventory arrival and the normal business operations. These circumstances are recognized by both parties as part of the normal risks associated with importing merchandise from China. Normally, such delays shall not suspend or reduce the Monthly Return or any other obligation of the business under this Agreement, except as specifically provided under Sections 4 and 5\.

**8\. Return of Investment**

At the end of the Term, **\[\[name of business\]\]** shall return the full Investment to the Investor within thirty \[30\] days, together with any Monthly Return that has become due and payable but remains unpaid.

**9\. Early Termination**

The Investor may request the return of the Investment before the end of the Term after completing at least twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. Such early termination shall be subject to written notice and reasonable coordination between both parties regarding the date and manner of repayment.

In the event of a medical, serious personal emergency, or other circumstances requiring the Investor to access the Investment funds, the Investor may request early termination before completing twelve (12) months. The Owner shall consider such requests in good faith and, where reasonably possible, accommodate the request based on the circumstances and the availability of funds.

Monthly Returns already paid to the Investor before an early termination shall belong to the Investor and shall not be deducted from the Investment. Monthly Returns shall stop accruing on the date the Investment is fully returned, and the Monthly Return for any partial month shall be pro-rated to that date. Any early termination and return of the Investment shall be documented in writing and agreed upon by both parties.

**10\. Nature of Arrangement; No Ownership or Management Rights**

The Monthly Return is a fixed amount agreed by the parties. The Investor's rights are limited to the Investment and the Monthly Returns expressly stated herein. Unless separately agreed upon in writing, this Agreement does not give the Investor ownership, voting, management, or decision-making rights in \[\[name of business\]\], nor any additional share of the business's profits.

**11\. Entire Agreement**

This Agreement represents the parties' entire agreement regarding the Investment and may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
